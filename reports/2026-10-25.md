## 1. 2027 status — headline

**2027 is NOT yet in Kia's online build-and-price configurator** for either
the K4 Hatchback (seriesId 2AC5) or the K4 sedan (2AC) — both still cap at
model year 2026. Models that **do** now have a 2027 configurator: Sorento,
Sportage, Niro, Telluride (+ Hybrid), Seltos, K5, Carnival (+ Hybrid),
Sportage Hybrid/PHEV, EV3.

**But 2027 K4 Hatchbacks are already physically on Texas lots** — the
configurator is just behind. Within 100 mi of College Station there are **64
MY2027 K4 Hatchbacks** in dealer stock/in-transit (confirmed by VIN position
10 = `V`), split GT-Line (34) and GT-Line Turbo (30). No 2027 EX units yet.

**Her interior combination is still not available, even on 2027.** Pulled
and verified window stickers on four MY2027 GT-Line units (opt codes
`2AC3255/15` and `2AC3255/17`) — identical option codes to MY2026, same two
choices: black-and-white (`/15`) or black-and-red (`/17`), both ventilated.
No solid black or solid gray ventilated interior has appeared for 2027. The
2026 configurator split is unchanged too: EX offers solid "Black SynTex"
but can't be ventilated; GT-Line/GT-Line Turbo are ventilated-capable but
only in the white or red accent trims.

**K4 sedan**: unchanged. Sedan EX offers a "Gray & Black SynTex Seat Trim"
the hatchback EX still does not — but sedan EX likewise cannot be
ventilated, so it doesn't solve her ask either.

**Bottom line: no change in her favor. The combo she wants (solid black or
gray + ventilated) does not exist on the hatchback in MY2026 or, now
confirmed, MY2027.**
